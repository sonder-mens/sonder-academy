The story.

Sonder was built on education. Not marketing, not strategy — education first. Eight locations and a reputation that speaks for itself came after. All of it a byproduct of a team that was highly trained and deeply invested in their craft.

The quality education that should have been available from day one didn't exist. So it was built from the ground up — real knowledge, taught by people still working behind the chair every day.

Sonder Academy is that education made available to everyone. No gatekeeping, no filler. Just everything we wish someone had handed us from the start.
